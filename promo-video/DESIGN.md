# Life Pawnshop Promo Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Vertical short video in a moody narrative pixel-game language: tiny female pawnshop keeper, dark old counter, burgundy shadows, muted-gold lantern pixels, sparse UI overlays, and result cards that feel like in-game settlement screens rather than ads. The tone is sly, intimate, and discussable: viewers should want to argue whether they would get the good, bad, or neutral ending.

## Colors

- Background: `#0b0a0f` ink-black with warm burgundy tint.
- Panel: `#18131a` and `#241a14` old wood / night interior.
- Foreground: `#f2f1ee` warm off-white.
- Accent: `#e8b45a` muted pawnshop gold.
- Danger/Bad ending: `#d95b4f` dried vermilion.
- Neutral ending: `#9f9588` worn ledger gray.
- Good ending: `#7fc79b` low-saturation jade.

## Typography

- Chinese captions/body: `Deng V` / `YaHei V` in regular or medium weights; avoid heavy black blocks and hard outlines.
- Pawnshop signage only: `Kai V`, used sparingly for the hanging shop sign, not for long captions.
- Cover title may use a slightly cute/trendy display face such as `FZYao V`, but should stay solid, soft-shadowed, and readable.
- Numbers and result labels use tabular numerals where relevant.

## Motion

- Primary transition: soft black-dip / small lantern glow between gameplay clips.
- Result-card reveal: stamp hit, short scale pop, then settle.
- Keeper expression changes are tiny sprite swaps, not smooth character animation.
- No long feature walkthroughs; every scene must either create curiosity or show a real interaction.

## What Not To Do

- Do not end on a large CTA ad card.
- Do not use polished anime portrait art for the keeper.
- Do not add detailed facial features; keep the keeper small-pixel and expression-led.
- Do not present good/bad/neutral endings as moral judgment.
- Do not copy specific characters from existing films or games; borrow only broad atmosphere and archetype.
- Do not use hard hollow title text, thick black outlines, or compressed subtitle boxes that make the video feel cheap.
